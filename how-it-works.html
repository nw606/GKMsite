How It Works

Three steps between your closet and your cash. No sorting, no spreadsheets, no waiting for your cards to sell.

1

Tell us about your cards

A short description, or a few photos, is all we need. Rough size (shoebox, three binders, etc.), anything you know about them. Don't know? That's fine, that's our job.

2

Ship them (or bring them to Berkeley)

We email you a prepaid label for most collections. You pack, we pay. Local? Drop off by appointment in Berkeley, CA. We send simple packaging guidelines so everything arrives safely.

3

Approve your appraisal, get paid

You receive a line-by-line report at fair market value. Approve it, and we pay within 48 hours — by your choice of payment method. Not happy? We return it.
