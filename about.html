About Good Karma Magic

A small Berkeley operation that buys Magic collections the way we'd want ours bought: honestly, promptly, and with the whole collection in mind.

Why we're different

Most buylists cherry-pick: they want the chase rares and hand your bulk commons back with a shipping bill. We built the opposite business. Send the whole collection — binders, longboxes, the shoebox of commons you never sorted — and we appraise all of it, line by line, at fair market value.

You see exactly how we reached your number in a transparent report. You approve it, we pay within 48 hours. If the appraisal isn't what you hoped, we ship everything back at our cost. No consignment, no "someday when it sells."

And because a business should give more than it takes, a share of our proceeds from every purchase goes to the Computer Technologies Program.

The practical bits

Where: Berkeley, CA — mail-in anywhere in the US, or local drop-off by appointment.
What we buy: any era, any condition, any size — from 50 cards to whole estates.
Payment: within 48 hours of approving your appraisal.
