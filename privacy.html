Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

Good Karma Magic ("we", "us") buys Magic: The Gathering collections. This policy explains what we collect when you use this site or ask for an appraisal, and what we do with it. The short version: we collect what we need to appraise your cards and pay you, and we don't sell your information to anyone.

What we collect

  • Contact details — your name, email address, and any other way you choose to reach us, used to respond to your inquiry and complete a sale.
  • Collection information — descriptions and photos of the cards you want to sell, used only to prepare your appraisal.
  • Transaction details — shipping address and payment details when a sale is agreed, used to send you packaging, receive your cards, and pay you.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your personal information with marketers.
  • We don't run advertising trackers on this site.
  • We don't publish your collection, photos, or correspondence. If we ever want to use an appraisal as a public example, we'll ask first and anonymize it.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to operate: email delivery for the contact form, and a payment service (such as PayPal or a bank transfer) when you choose how to be paid. Those providers handle your data under their own privacy policies and only to the extent needed to complete the service.

Retention

We keep appraisal correspondence while a sale is being discussed or completed. If a sale doesn't go ahead, you can ask us to delete your details at any time. Completed records needed for tax and accounting purposes are kept as required by law.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us through the contact page.

Cookies

This site does not use tracking cookies. Any storage kept by your browser is for the site's basic operation only.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
