Sealed Watch List

The sealed market is in a correction. Prices peaked one to two months ago and Rudy expects volatile prices for the next 3 to 12 months, with a downturn through the end of the year and a possible rebound in January or February. Reprints are the main risk, Lorwyn already collapsed on one and a Final Fantasy reprint lands in December. That is why this list watches for local lows instead of chasing highs, with Star Trek launching November 13 into soft distributor demand.
