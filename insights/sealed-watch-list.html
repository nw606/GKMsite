Sealed Watch List

The sealed market is in a correction. Prices peaked one to two months ago and Rudy expects volatile prices for the next 3 to 12 months, with a downturn through the end of the year and a possible rebound in January or February. For a buyer that is good news. Reprints are the main opportunity, not the main risk. Lorwyn already fell on one and a Final Fantasy reprint lands in December, and each reprint pushes prices toward the local low this list is watching for. Star Trek launches November 13 into soft distributor demand.

Last updated 2026-11-10.

  • Nothing here is a buy recommendation. Prices move fast after release, and a predicted low is a forecast, not a promise.
  • Sentiment is a 5-point rating from Loved to Hated for the set overall, not the individual product, so all products from the same set carry the same rating. Loved means strong sustained demand and positive reception, Liked means solid demand and generally positive reception, Mixed means conflicting signals such as decent sales with a price collapse or reprint hangover, Disliked means weak demand or a notable fall from preorder with negative seller reception, Hated means sellers are losing money with worst-in-years reception. It is a judgment call, reviewed at the weekly update.
