Sealed Watch List

Sealed Magic products we are watching for local low prices before they appreciate. The goal is to predict where the bottom will be on products already decided worth owning, so the buy happens near the low instead of after it. A product earns a row only if it has a real appreciation case: set quality, Commander demand, play demand, or consumption that removes supply over time.

Coverage: every set released in the past year, plus products we have identified as likely to gain in value even when their set is older. Set size is the number of cards in the set, from Scryfall. Prices are last known with their date, not live quotes. A predicted local low is a price and a rough timeframe with the basis stated, and it gets checked against the actual lowest price reached once the window passes. Buy threshold is the price that still clears our 50 percent ROI hurdle after fees and shipping. Missing prices show as n/a.
