Sealed Watch List

The sealed market is in a correction. Prices peaked one to two months ago and Rudy expects volatile prices for the next 3 to 12 months, with a downturn through the end of the year and a possible rebound in January or February. For a buyer that is good news. Reprints are the main opportunity, not the main risk. Lorwyn already fell on one and a Final Fantasy reprint lands in December, and each reprint pushes prices toward the local low this list is watching for. Star Trek launches November 13 into soft distributor demand.

Last updated 2026-11-07.
