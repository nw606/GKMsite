Verified sellers

Sellers and stores we trust for sealed Magic product. Marketplaces are platforms where our buy links regularly check out. Every individual listing still gets our five-check verification (exact-edition title, photos confirm the edition, specifics agree, description consistent, sane pricing). Direct stores are listed two ways: buy-link verified means a purchase link from them passed our five checks, community recommended means they were recommended by MTG players and we vetted them. Distributors sell wholesale to business accounts rather than retail. New distributor accounts typically require a business license and a physical storefront, and wholesale pricing is often not below internet retail. Each entry notes free shipping, sales tax, and tax-exemption policy where the store states it. The list is rechecked periodically and entries fall off when they stop checking out.

Last updated 2026-11-01.
