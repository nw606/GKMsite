What is the right price for a Magic Star Trek play booster box before release

Star Trek Play booster boxes Sealed product Buying sealed

Joel are Magic asked a simple question in a short this week. What is the right price for a Star Trek box? It sounds like a throwaway, but ten days before release it is actually the question, because the numbers for this set are all over the place.

Here is what I found when I looked. A Star Trek play booster box holds 30 packs at 6.99 dollars each, which puts the sticker price at 209.70 dollars. Preorder listings are nowhere near that. Store trackers have play boxes from around 116 dollars to around 130 dollars, roughly 40 percent under sticker, while collector boxes sit over sticker. The set releases November 13, with prerelease events running November 6 to 12, so these are still preorder prices and they can move.

The catch is that a cheap box is not automatically a good buy. Say you grab one at 120 dollars and try to flip it. A standard TCGplayer seller pays 10.75 percent commission plus a 2.5 percent transaction fee and 30 cents per order, which lands around 13.5 percent all in. Sell that box for 130 dollars and you keep roughly 112 dollars before shipping and packaging. If you paid anywhere near 120 dollars for it, there is nothing left. You did all the work for a rounding error.

So the right price depends on which side of the counter you are on. If you want to draft or crack packs with friends, a play box well under sticker before release is a fine deal, and waiting for release week might shave a little more off. If you are buying to resell, do the fee math first and be honest about it. A box that looks cheap next to its sticker price is not a deal until it clears fees, shipping, and your time with room to spare.
