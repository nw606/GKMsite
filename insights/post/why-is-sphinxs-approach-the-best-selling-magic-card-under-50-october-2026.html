Why is Sphinx's Approach the best selling Magic card under $50 in October 2026

Market analysis Reality Fracture Singles prices

TCGplayer's October best sellers are out, and the card at the top of the under-$50 list is not a chase mythic or a Commander staple. It is Sphinx's Approach, a common from Reality Fracture, at a $1.26 average sale price.

The reason is printed right on the card. Sphinx's Approach says your deck can have any number of copies, so a player building around it does not buy four. They buy twenty or thirty. That is the whole trick, and it is why a card that sells for about a dollar can move more copies than anything else on the marketplace.

There is a second lesson in the price. The card opened around $2.06 on release day and fell to about $0.58 by early November as supply caught up, which is the normal shape for a new common. The sales did not fall with it. People kept buying while the price dropped, because the demand comes from the deck, not from speculation. The enabler got cheaper while its payoff got expensive. Sphinx of the Chimes foil went from $0.27 to $7.43 in the same stretch, since exiling four copies of Approach from your graveyard puts a Sphinx from your deck straight into play.

For anyone selling cards, the takeaway is simple. Velocity lives at a dollar. A $1.26 card that sells hundreds of copies is worth more shelf space than a $50 card that sells twice. And for anyone sorting bulk, any-number commons are the ones to pull out first, because their demand is structural. It does not need a spike to show up.

Sources: TCGplayer's top selling Magic cards of October 2026 and GameRant on the Sphinx of the Chimes spike.
