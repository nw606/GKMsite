Are Nauctis play booster boxes a good buy with the Sunken Treasures box topper

Market analysis Sealed product Play booster boxes

Louie at Kitchen Table TCG thinks Nauctis is going to be a home run. His store had its worst prerelease in five years with Star Trek, and he sees the new set as the reset, a return to Magic's own world after a run of Universes Beyond.

The center of his argument is the box topper. Every Nauctis play box ships with a Sunken Treasures topper, one of 20 pearlescent artifact cards, and Louie puts the average topper at 15 to 20 dollars. That guaranteed card puts a floor under the box and gives stores a second thing to sell when they crack boxes. Star Trek had no topper, and he says one would have saved its play boxes.

That last part is hard to argue with. Star Trek play boxes trade around 104 dollars, below the 121.50 dollar distributor cost. A 15 dollar topper would not have fixed the set, but it would have softened the landing.

The 15 to 20 dollar number is where I slow down. Only one topper is confirmed so far, a Crucible of Worlds reprint, and the other 19 are still unrevealed. A topper pool lives or dies on its weakest cards, and an average needs the whole list. Until the full 20 are out, 15 dollars is a hope, not a floor.

The same caution applies to his Collector box call. Nauctis Collector packs drop from 15 cards to 12, and the box keeps its 323.88 dollar list price. Louie thinks the EV still comes out slightly higher because scarcer common and uncommon foils get more valuable. Maybe. But fewer cards at the same price means the EV has to work harder, and that is a guess about foil prices.

The takeaway is simpler than either claim. Nauctis play boxes have a real shot at holding value because the topper gives them something Star Trek never had, but do not pay a premium for it until the full list is revealed. If the pool is strong, the floor Louie describes is real. If it is not, wait for release week pricing.

Sources: Kitchen Table TCG on YouTube and Collecting Nauctis: The Sunken Realm.
