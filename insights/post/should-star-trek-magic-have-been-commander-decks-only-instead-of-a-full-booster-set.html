Should Star Trek Magic have been Commander decks only instead of a full booster set

Star Trek Universes Beyond Sealed product

Louie at Kitchen Table TCG in Hurricane, West Virginia usually fills about 40 seats on a Friday prerelease night. For Star Trek he got 12. Saturday drew 8 against a recent average of about 25, Sunday held at 10. The whole weekend pulled fewer players than a normal Friday.

It was not just his store. Mayhem Comics in Iowa counted 62 players, the worst prerelease of the year there, while every in-universe set drew over 110. Brute Force Games in San Diego called it their lowest in 10 years. A store in Turin expected about a tenth of the Reality Fracture turnout. Same story in four places.

Louie's read is that Wizards overestimated how much Magic players care about Star Trek, and the same thing happened with Spider-Man and Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles. Fantasy-adjacent Universes Beyond like Avatar and Lord of the Rings can carry a full set. These three could not, and he says they should have followed the Warhammer and Fallout model, Commander decks plus Collector Boosters and no Play Boosters.

The shelves back him up. His Federation Fleet and We Are the Borg decks moved, while Landing Party still sits, odd since it has the strongest reprint value of the four at about $84 against a $51 market price. Play boxes keep sliding, TCGplayer market at $110.02, below the $115 to $125 low and the $121.50 a distributor charged one buyer. Collector boxes look steadier in the $400 to $500 range, helped by the autographed cards.

One more wrinkle. Scene Bundles, Draft Nights, and some Commander decks are still delayed from distributors, with about 30 pre-orders stuck. Soft demand is the story, but some product people did want never arrived. Wizards has already said 2027 swings back in-universe, and that looks like the right call.

If you are buying Star Trek sealed, the prerelease numbers say the same thing as the prices. Play boxes are cheap because nobody is showing up to play the set, not because the market missed something. Commander decks are the safer corner, but check the reprint value before you pay up.
