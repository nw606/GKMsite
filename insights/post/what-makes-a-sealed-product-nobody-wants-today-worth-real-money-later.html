What makes a sealed product nobody wants today worth real money later

Market analysis Sealed product Star Trek

Frank's Hobbies has a new video. The sealed market is fluid, and the product everyone mocks today can be the one people chase in a few years. His example is Pokemon Steam Siege, a 2016 set whose packs were turning up at Dollar Tree for a dollar and now sell for real money.

The numbers back him up. A sealed Steam Siege booster pack carries a TCGplayer market price of 35.19 dollars as of November 21, up 75 percent in a year. The same pack bottomed at 7.73 dollars in April 2024. A sealed booster box sits at 1,437 dollars, about 40 dollars a pack.

One honest wrinkle. The Dollar Tree packs were three-card mini packs, not the full ten-card packs priced at 35.19 dollars, so a dollar to 35 dollars is not quite the same item. The direction is still real, and packs now sit at their highest price since tracking began in February 2024.

So why did it happen. Steam Siege went out of print in 2016 and never came back, so every pack opened or tucked away shrinks what is left. Only three cards in the set beat the price of a pack, led by a Gardevoir EX at 109 dollars, and opening one returns about 6 percent of what the pack costs. The value is the sealed pack, not the gamble inside.

Fluid cuts both ways, though. The same reading list has Alpha Investments on Pokemon sealed falling 60 to 70 percent in about 60 days on heavy reprints and panic selling. Steam Siege survived because nobody reprinted it, and one reprint kills the scarcity story overnight.

That is the test to run on Magic. Star Trek play boxes trade at 109.78 dollars, below the 121.50 dollar distributor cost. The question is not whether people like the set right now. It is whether supply is truly finite, whether anyone will still want it in five years, and whether a reprint is off the table. If the answer to all three is yes, the unloved box is worth holding. If any answer is no, walk away.

Sources: Frank's Hobbies on YouTube, Alpha Investments on YouTube, TickerMint Steam Siege Booster Pack price page, and the Sealed Watch List.
