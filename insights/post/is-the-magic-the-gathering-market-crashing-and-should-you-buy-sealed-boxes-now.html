Is the Magic the Gathering market crashing and should you buy sealed boxes now

Market downturn Sealed product Play booster boxes

Alpha Investments put out an hour-long video this week arguing the trading card downturn is picking up speed. Rudy says Pokemon peaked four to five months ago and Magic peaked one to two months ago, and he expects volatile prices for the next three to twelve months. His best guess is a downturn through the end of the year with a rebound in January or February. He frames it as a healthy correction that flushes out flippers, leveraged buyers, and weak stores.

His sharpest warning is about Final Fantasy. Play booster boxes sit around 250 dollars by his count, and he expects them to fall to 110 to 140 dollars when a reprint lands in December. When I checked, MTGGoldfish lists the sealed play box at about 290 dollars, down 2 percent on the week, so the slide is already underway. He tells a similar story on Star Trek, where a distributor charged a patron 121.50 dollars per play box and the buyer could not resell online without losing thousands. That matches what we found this week, with Star Trek play boxes already trading well under sticker before release.

The part worth taking seriously is the reprint risk. Rudy points to Lorwyn collapsing on a reprint and warns Strixhaven and Tarkir could be next. But he also warns against calling every set dead, because Murders at Karlov Manor and Foundations were both mocked when cheap and both recovered once out of print. Recent sealed that can still be reprinted is the riskiest thing to hold, and out of print product is where the recovery stories come from.

If you hold Final Fantasy boxes, the December reprint is the thing to watch. If you are buying, keep cash ready and pay up only for product that cannot be reprinted or is already deeply discounted. And if you are selling into this, do not panic out of out of print stock just because the market is red. Downturns are when patient buyers get paid, but only if they are not buying with borrowed money.
