Why are Magic Star Trek play booster boxes selling for half price

Market analysis Star Trek Play booster boxes

A Magic: The Gathering | Star Trek play booster box cost $105.30 on TCGplayer this morning. The box holds thirty packs at a $6.99 MSRP, so the whole box should cost $209.70. It is selling for about half that, three days after release.

The rest of the set looks the same way. The collector booster display is $403.45, down from a $694.74 preorder level. All four commander decks sell below their $74.99 list price, with We Are the Borg the priciest of the four at $71.35 and Klingon Fury near $30 before release. The set as a whole has lost almost a quarter of its value in a week, according to TCGIndex.

The awkward part is what stores paid. Distributors charged about $121.50 a box, which is more than the box sells for now. After marketplace fees and shipping, a store selling at $105 is down roughly $35 a box. Prerelease turnout was soft too, with one San Diego store calling it the lowest in ten years, and early Arena play ran about half the usual volume.

This is not a singles problem. The priciest regular card in the set, Captain Jean-Luc Picard, sits under $20, and the chase foils fell 15 to 82 percent in the week before release as more packs got opened. The set just has more supply than demand, and the supply arrived all at once.

For buyers, the lesson is to wait. Boxes below distributor cost do not usually stay there, but nothing in the first three days says the bottom is in yet. The decks are the clearer buy right now, since even the priciest one is under list price and the cheapest is less than half of it. For sellers holding sealed Star Trek, the next few weeks will tell whether this is a slow start or the real price.

Sources: TCGIndex Star Trek set value, Nerdsnack on the commander decks, Nerdsnack on the chase cards, Fandom Pulse on the launch, and the GKM sealed watch list.
