Should you buy Magic Star Trek singles before release or wait for prices to fall

Star Trek Singles prices Buying singles Reprints

Joel are Magic spent most of his latest mailbag on one set. Star Trek releases November 13, prerelease runs this weekend, and his viewers wanted to know three things. Did Wizards load the set with all ten shock lands and signed cards because they worried it would not sell. Should you buy Star Trek singles before release or wait. And is the set going to flop. His answers were partly yes, wait, and no.

The prices back up the wait part. All four Commander decks sell for 62 to 67 dollars on TCGplayer against a 74.99 dollar sticker price, checked November 3. But the value inside is not even. Draftsim put Landing Party reprints at about 84 dollars and We Are the Borg at about 60, while Klingon Fury came in around 32 and Federation Fleet around 29. Two of the four decks hold less in reprints than the box costs.

The shocks are the bigger story for singles buyers. All ten appear in Play Boosters in the LCARS treatment, and a full reprint at that rarity usually means prices soften once packs start opening on November 13. The chase cards stay out of Play Boosters entirely. Signed cards, Klingon script cards, and the surge foil LCARS and Enterprise versions only show up in Collector Boosters. So Play Booster supply will be heavy on the lands and light on the top-end cards.

Joel also flagged a pricing trap worth remembering. A Chatterfang raised foil is listed at 2,000 dollars while the last sale was 890. That gap is the difference between TCGplayer low and market, and it is why a list price is not a sale price. If you are selling into release week, price against recent sales, not the highest listing.

The takeaway is simple. Play at prerelease this weekend and hold off on singles until the market settles after release. If you want a Commander deck, pick the one you will actually play and check the reprint value first. The set is not a flop, but the early prices say wait a week.
