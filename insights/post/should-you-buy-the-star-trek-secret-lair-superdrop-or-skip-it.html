Should you buy the Star Trek Secret Lair superdrop or skip it

Market analysis Star Trek Buying sealed

MTG Metrics put out his buy or skip video this morning, hours before the Star Trek Secret Lair superdrop goes live at 9am Pacific. His verdict is short. Buy it if you are a die-hard Trek fan, and think twice otherwise. Five drops, each 29.99 dollars nonfoil or 39.99 foil, 149.95 for the full set in nonfoil, and a sixth drop still unrevealed.

The numbers back up the caution. Lair Index, which tracks Secret Lair prices against TCGplayer, puts the most valuable card in the whole superdrop at 15.62 dollars. That is Tendi, Exploring the Future, from the Lower Decks drop, and it is the price of the cheapest other printing, not the Secret Lair version. The next two are Beam Us Up at 6.23 and Boimler at 5.66, both also Lower Decks. So the value is bunched into one of the five drops, and the other four are mostly art and theme. The Klingon language drop and the Khan drop are fun ideas, and fun ideas do not pay for themselves on the secondary market. Wizards sweetens the pot a little with a foil Transporter Boondoggle for every 199 dollars spent, and shipping is free over 99, which helps if you are buying the full set.

The context matters too. The main Star Trek set lands November 13, and play boxes are already trading below distributor cost, which tells you the set is not short on supply. A Secret Lair tied to a set nobody is chasing for value is a fan purchase, not a flip. If you love Lower Decks or you want those reversible transporter cards on your table, 29.99 for the drop you actually want is fair. If you are buying all five hoping to resell, skip it and put the 150 dollars toward singles after release, when the set has had a week to settle.
