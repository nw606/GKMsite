Daily Reading List - November 24, 2026

reading-list

New articles and videos from around the MTG world today.

New articles

No new articles on the blog today.

New videos

  • What happened to the STAR TREK COLLAPSE ? (Alpha Investments) - Star Trek Play boxes fell to about 95 dollars, bounced back to about 111 dollars, and the video blames Hasbro selling its own product on Amazon below distributor cost, with a wait-and-see verdict until after the Black Friday sales.
  • How this STAR WARS card game FAILED! (Frank's Hobbies) - Star Wars: Destiny ran from 2016 to about 2020 before Fantasy Flight stopped production, and the video argues custom dice made it too costly to sustain, though a loyal fan base keeps the game alive.
  • The TCG "Race to the Bottom" (Kitchen Table TCG) - Star Trek boxes are selling below cost because stores are out of space and need the cash, with losses near 40 dollars a box after TCGplayer fees.
  • 16 Cards Flying in Price, and the 11 to Sell (Joel are Magic) - Names 16 cards climbing in price and flags 11 to sell now, timed to a Black Friday fee-free Card Conduit window.
  • Buying Cheap Trading Cards Isn't Always a Good Deal… (Leumas Card Craze) - Day eight of the list-everything experiment warns that cheap bulk can lose money once duplicates, damaged cards, processing time, and storage are counted.
  • The Rise of the Machines is Destroying the Bulk Market (Card N All Gaming) - TCGplayer Roca sifters let anyone list bulk fast, flooding TCGplayer with listings and pushing prices down, with Dunsparce listings jumping from 177 to 1,460 while its price fell to 1.17 dollars, and the same flooding hitting Magic too.
