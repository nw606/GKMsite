Is TCG inventory software worth paying for when you sell trading cards

Selling cards Inventory software TCG business Leumas Live

Leumas Card Craze is building his own selling software, Leumas Live, and in this video he stops to ask a question a lot of builders skip. If someone else had built the same tool, would he actually pay for it? That is a better test than a feature list, because it forces you to say what the tool is worth in real work, not in theory.

He is coming at it from a big pile of cardboard. He manages about 150,000 trading cards through his shop, and he says the value is in time, organization, and reliable processes. That rings true. At that scale a small snag repeated a thousand times is not a small snag anymore. A tool that saves a few seconds on every listing, or stops you hunting for a card you know you own, pays for itself in a way a prettier dashboard never will.

When I looked closer, the honest part is that value looks different for every seller. This is day 14 of a 21 part series, so he is still building in public and still setting standards for what Leumas Live should do. A solo seller drowning in intake needs something different from a store with staff and a steady flow of orders. The same monthly price can be a bargain for one and a waste for the other, and that is why a demo that looks slick can still be the wrong buy.

So before you pay for inventory software, track where your time actually goes for a week. Is it listing, repricing, finding cards, or packing? If a tool does not fix the step you repeat most, skip it, even if everyone else loves it. If it does fix that step, the price matters a lot less than the hours it gives back. Buy the boring improvement, not the impressive one.
