How do card stores survive an 18-month TCG bear market

Market analysis Sealed product

Louie at Kitchen Table TCG runs a game store in West Virginia, and he thinks the market just turned. After 18 to 24 months of a tear in trading cards, he calls the next 18 months a downturn, with three moves for stores that want to keep winning while prices fall.

First, cut buy rates. Vendors at Collecticon paid 85 percent cash for cards, and Louie says a card can drop 15 percent overnight, so anyone who paid that much and held Pokemon through the last two months is losing money after about 15 days. His shop never went above 80 percent, and he says the right level now is 70 percent cash, 75 percent on the high end.

Second, grow your distributor allocation while other stores walk away. Distribution has been locked down for two years, and spending more now buys a bigger allocation when the market turns. The risk is cash flow, and Star Trek is the warning. Boxes bought at 115 to 120 dollars are about 20 percent lower on TCGplayer, and with about 15 percent in fees that makes a 35 percent hole.

Third, chase sales velocity instead of margin. Sealed will be harder to move, so take 5 to 10 percent less margin if it grows volume. His proof is Bloomburrow. Two years ago distributors were calling to offer pallets, and today a Bloomburrow collector booster box sits around 1,349 dollars.

Star Trek proves the point. Play booster boxes trade around 104 dollars, below the 121.50 dollar distributor cost, so stores are selling below what they paid. Buy rates have to come down before more inventory gets bought at the top.

The takeaway depends on which side of the counter you are on. Selling cards? Expect lower cash offers, and sell what you do not want now instead of waiting for a better price. Buying sealed? Watch for stores that take his advice and start discounting to move volume. A bear market punishes anyone holding boxes at cost, but it is the best buying window in two years for anyone with cash and patience.

Sources: Kitchen Table TCG on YouTube and CardMetrics Bloomburrow prices.
