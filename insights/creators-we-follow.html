Creators we follow

The YouTube creators behind the daily reading list and much of the market coverage here. Generally trust means knowledgeable but not always right. Trust but verify means check a claim against sold prices before it touches a buying decision. Each entry links to the channel and to its YouTube RSS feed.

Generally trust

  • AnonTCG · Sealed product EV analysis, an EV tracker, and weekly distribution updates.
    Channel · RSS feed
  • Card N All Gaming · Louisville KY game store with a multi-TCG retailer margin perspective.
    Channel · RSS feed
  • Joel are Magic · MTG news, Commander coverage, anti-FOMO product guidance, and new-set demand signals.
    Channel · RSS feed
  • Final Trade · MTG finance, monthly market walks, and card-store buy disclosures. The highest-signal channel in this group.
    Channel · RSS feed
  • TCG Bulk Kings · Bulk value extraction, card sifting, chaos inventory, and TCGplayer selling operations.
    Channel · RSS feed

Trust but verify

  • Alpha Investments · Sealed investing, entertainment-forward. Calls from this channel can move prices, so treat them as narrative rather than data.
    Channel · RSS feed
  • Leumas Card Craze · Pokemon-focused reseller covering eBay bulk-selling experiments and tactics. Useful as a bulk-listing case study rather than an MTG market signal.
    Channel · RSS feed
  • Frank's Hobbies · Multi-TCG seller with market commentary. Newer to MTG with less track record there.
    Channel · RSS feed
  • eugigamez · Cross-TCG pack openings and cross-hobby ROI entertainment. Best read as a sentiment thermometer.
    Channel · RSS feed
  • MTG Metrics · Sealed product breakdowns and buyer guides, with set-by-set value picks.
    Channel · RSS feed
  • Golden Gate Pulls · TCGplayer seller running a real-numbers hobby-to-business experiment, tracking sealed-to-singles results with fees and ROI.
    Channel · RSS feed
